Director's Briefing & Architectural Learnings

Key findings from running persona evaluations against the dual MCP architecture:

  1. Headless Service Layer Parity: By enforcing that both web routes and AI agents call validate_application() and create_application() in PortalService, zero invalid applications can be created by conversational AI.
  2. Strict Role & Endpoint Isolation: Separating /mcp/citizen and /mcp/officer prevents prompt injection from enabling citizens to trigger administrative approvals.
  3. Statutory 15-Day SLA Guarantee: Applications are sorted strictly by SLA urgency (oldest pending first), and automatic deemed approval guarantees RTPS statutory rights.
